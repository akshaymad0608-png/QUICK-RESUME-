Resume Skills Section

The right skills, matched to the job description, are what ATS software and recruiters scan for first. Here’s what to include — and how.

Updated August 2026 · By QuickResume · Free · No sign-up

Quick answer

List a mix of hard skills (tools, languages, software) and a few relevant soft skills, and mirror the exact terms from the job description. Group them so they’re easy to scan.

Hard skills by role (examples)

  • Software engineering: Java, Python, C++, data structures, REST APIs, Git, unit testing, system design
  • Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, TypeScript, Tailwind, responsive design, web accessibility
  • Backend / DevOps: Node.js, Spring Boot, SQL, MongoDB, Docker, AWS, CI/CD, Linux
  • Data & analytics: SQL, Python, Excel, Power BI, Tableau, statistics, ETL, data cleaning
  • Digital marketing: SEO, Google Analytics 4, Google Ads, Meta Ads, content strategy, email marketing, keyword research, conversion tracking
  • Finance & accounts: Tally ERP, advanced Excel, GST filing, TDS, bank reconciliation, accounts payable, financial modelling, budgeting
  • Design: Figma, Adobe XD, Photoshop, Illustrator, prototyping, design systems, user research, wireframing
  • Human resources: recruitment, sourcing, onboarding, payroll, HRMS tools, employee engagement, performance reviews, labour compliance
  • Sales & business development: lead generation, CRM (Salesforce/Zoho), cold calling, negotiation, pipeline management, upselling, client onboarding, target achievement
  • Operations & supply chain: inventory management, vendor coordination, logistics, SAP, demand planning, process documentation, cost control, quality checks
  • Customer support: ticketing tools (Zendesk/Freshdesk), CRM, escalation handling, SLA management, product knowledge, live chat, call handling, complaint resolution
  • Teaching & education: lesson planning, curriculum design, classroom management, student assessment, digital teaching tools, parent communication, remedial teaching, record keeping
  • Nursing & healthcare: patient care, vital monitoring, medication administration, IV insertion, infection control, EMR documentation, emergency response, patient counselling
  • Civil & mechanical: AutoCAD, SolidWorks, project estimation, site supervision, quality assurance, BOQ preparation, safety compliance, MS Project

Soft skills that matter

  • Communication — writing clearly, explaining technical work to non-technical people, running a meeting
  • Problem-solving — breaking a vague problem down and choosing between imperfect options
  • Teamwork & collaboration — code reviews, handovers, working across departments
  • Ownership & accountability — following a task to completion without being chased
  • Adaptability — picking up a new tool or process quickly when priorities shift
  • Time management — prioritising when everything is marked urgent
  • Attention to detail — catching the error before it reaches the customer
  • Leadership — mentoring juniors, coordinating a small team, driving a decision
Always match your skills to the specific job posting. ATS software often scores resumes on keyword overlap with the job description.

Frequently asked questions

What skills should I put on my resume?
A mix of hard skills (tools, languages, software) and a few relevant soft skills, all matched to the specific job description.

How many skills should a resume have?
Around 8–15 relevant skills, grouped for easy scanning — quality and relevance beat a long, generic list.

Should I list soft skills?
A few, yes — but only ones you can demonstrate with examples in your experience. Recruiters value proof over buzzwords.
