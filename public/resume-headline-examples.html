Resume Headline Examples for Naukri

A resume headline is the one-line summary at the top that tells a recruiter who you are in five seconds. Here are examples you can copy and adapt.

Updated August 2026 · By QuickResume · Free · No sign-up

Quick answer

A great headline names your role + years/level + a strength or specialisation. Example: “Frontend Developer · 3 years · React & TypeScript, performance-focused”. Use it at the very top of your resume.

Resume headline for a Naukri profile

On Naukri the headline is a profile field, not just a line on your CV — it is what recruiters read in the search listing before they open anything. The field holds 250 characters — Naukri shows a live counter as you type. Far less than that survives in the listing: roughly the first 120 on desktop, closer to 80 on mobile, and the only part you can truly rely on is the first 60. Front-load the role and your strongest keyword; anything after that is a bonus.

Recruiters search Naukri by keyword. A headline that says “Hardworking professional seeking growth” matches nothing. One that says “Java Developer | 4 yrs | Spring Boot, Microservices, AWS” matches every search a Java hiring manager runs.

The formula that works on Naukri

Naukri headline guides converge on the same shape, separated by pipes so each part reads as a distinct keyword:

Formula

[Role] | [years, or degree + year] | [3–6 core skills] | [domain or stack] | [notice period or location]

The last slot is the one most people leave out. In Indian hiring the notice period is often the first thing a recruiter filters on — putting “30-day notice” or “immediate joiner” in the headline saves a message and moves you up the shortlist.

When a recruiter searches the Naukri database they see a stacked list: name, current designation, experience, location, key skills, and your headline. Your headline is the only line in that row you fully control.

What to avoid in a Naukri headline

  • “Seeking a challenging role in a reputed organisation” — the most common headline on Naukri, and it contains not one term a recruiter would ever search for.
  • Biodata fields — age, marital status, date of birth. They belong nowhere near a headline and waste the characters that decide whether you get opened.
  • Emojis, stars, arrows and ALL CAPS — they do not make you stand out in a text list, and creative formatting can be invisible to parsing.
  • Job-description copy-paste — writing what the role does instead of what you have done.
  • Inflated experience — the number in your headline is checked against the number in your profile.
  • A stale headline — one that still says “fresher” two years in, or names a stack you left behind.

Frequently asked questions

What should a fresher write as a Naukri headline?

Degree and year, your two strongest skills, and the role you want — for example “B.Tech CSE 2026 · Java, DSA & React · Open to SDE roles”. Skip phrases like “hardworking” that no recruiter searches for.

What is the character limit for a Naukri resume headline?

The field holds 250 characters. Only about the first 120 appear in the recruiter search listing, and fewer on mobile, so put the role and your strongest keyword first.

What is a resume headline?
A one-line summary at the top of your resume stating your role, level and a key strength — it helps recruiters place you instantly.

What is a good resume headline for freshers?
Name your degree/field, 1–2 top skills, and the role you want, e.g. “Computer Science Graduate · Java & React · Seeking SDE role”.

How long should a resume headline be?
Keep it under about 12 words — one scannable line with your strongest keyword first.
