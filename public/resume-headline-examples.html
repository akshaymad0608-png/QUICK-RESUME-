Resume Headline Examples

A resume headline is the one-line summary at the top that tells a recruiter who you are in five seconds. Here are examples you can copy and adapt.

Updated August 2026 · By QuickResume · Free · No sign-up

Quick answer

A great headline names your role + years/level + a strength or specialisation. Example: “Frontend Developer · 3 years · React & TypeScript, performance-focused”. Use it at the very top of your resume.

Resume headline examples for freshers

  • Computer Science Graduate · Skilled in Java, DSA & React · Seeking SDE role
  • B.Com Fresher · Strong in Accounting & Advanced Excel · Detail-oriented
  • Mechanical Engineering Graduate · AutoCAD & SolidWorks · Quick learner
  • Recent Marketing Graduate · SEO, Content & Social Media · Data-driven

Resume headline examples for experienced professionals

  • Senior Frontend Developer · 6 years · React, TypeScript & Design Systems
  • Digital Marketing Manager · 5 years · Grew organic traffic 3× via SEO
  • Data Analyst · 4 years · SQL, Python & Power BI · Retail & Finance
  • Project Manager · PMP-certified · 8 years delivering SaaS on time & budget
Match your headline to the job title in the posting, keep it under ~12 words, and put your strongest keyword first.

Frequently asked questions

What is a resume headline?
A one-line summary at the top of your resume stating your role, level and a key strength — it helps recruiters place you instantly.

What is a good resume headline for freshers?
Name your degree/field, 1–2 top skills, and the role you want, e.g. “Computer Science Graduate · Java & React · Seeking SDE role”.

How long should a resume headline be?
Keep it under about 12 words — one scannable line with your strongest keyword first.
