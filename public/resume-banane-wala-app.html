Resume Banane Wala App: Mobile Se Free Resume Kaise Banaye

App install kiye bina, apne phone se hi ek saaf, ATS-friendly resume banane ka aasan tareeka.

Updated October 2026 · By QuickResume · Free · No sign-up

Quick answer

Mobile se resume banane ke liye app install karna zaroori nahi. Browser mein ek free online resume builder kholo, template chuno, details bharo aur PDF download kar lo. Fresher ke liye ek page ka, saaf aur ATS-friendly resume sabse accha rehta hai.

QuickResume doosre tareeke wala tool hai: ye ek website hai jo seedhe mobile browser mein khulti hai, isliye alag app install karne ki zaroorat nahi. Basic use ke liye sign-up bhi zaroori nahi hai.

Mobile se resume banane ke steps

  1. Browser kholo (Chrome ya koi bhi) aur quickresume.business par jao, phir "Start" dabao.
  2. Template chuno. 60+ templates hain. Fresher ho to Fresher ya Student category dekho, jo education aur projects ko pehle dikhati hai.
  3. Details bharo: naam, phone, email, education, projects ya internship, aur skills. Har step form jaisa hai, isliye chhoti screen par bhi aasan hai.
  4. AI se madad lo: summary likhwao, ya apne simple points ko behtar bullet points mein badlwao. Phir khud padh ke apne hisaab se theek karo.
  5. ATS score check karo aur jo suggestions mile unhe theek karo.
  6. PDF download karo. File seedhe phone mein save ho jati hai, aur aap use WhatsApp, email ya job portal par bhej sakte ho.

Fresher ke liye zaroori tips

  • Resume 1 page ka rakho. Zyada experience nahi hai to ek page kaafi hai. Detail ke liye resume kitna lamba hona chahiye padho.
  • Education, projects aur skills upar rakho agar job experience nahi hai.
  • Simple, ek-column layout chuno taaki Naukri jaise portals aur company ke ATS use aasani se padh sakein.
  • Sirf wahi likho jo sach ho. Skills aur marks galat likhne se interview mein dikkat ho sakti hai.
  • PDF ka naam saaf rakho, jaise Rahul_Sharma_Resume.pdf, "resume final 2.pdf" nahi.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Resume banane wala sabse accha app kaunsa hai?
Aapki zaroorat par depend karta hai. Agar aap koi app install nahi karna chahte, to mobile browser mein chalne wala free resume builder jaise QuickResume aasan option hai. Koi bhi tool chuno, ye dekho ki PDF free mein download hota hai ya nahi, aur resume ATS-friendly hai ya nahi.

Kya mobile se free resume ban sakta hai?
Haan. QuickResume mobile browser mein khulta hai, template chun kar details bhar sakte ho, aur PDF phone mein download hota hai. Basic use ke liye sign-up zaroori nahi hai.

Fresher resume mein kya kya likhna chahiye?
Naam aur contact details, ek chhoti summary ya objective, education, projects ya internship, skills, aur zaroorat ho to certifications. Sab ek page mein aur saaf format mein.

Resume PDF mein banana chahiye ya Word mein?
Zyadatar jagah PDF behtar hai kyunki format sab devices par same rehta hai. Agar job portal Word file maangta hai, to wahi bhejo. Dhyan rakho ki PDF text wala ho, photo ya scan wala nahi.
