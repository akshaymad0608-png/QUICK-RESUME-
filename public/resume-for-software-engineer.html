Software Engineer Resume

A great developer resume proves impact with metrics and the right tech keywords. Here’s the format, skills and bullet examples that get callbacks.

Updated August 2026 · By QuickResume · Free · No sign-up

Quick answer

Lead with a summary + tech stack, then experience with measurable bullets (“cut API latency 30%”), projects with links, and skills matched to the job. Keep it one page and ATS-friendly.

What to include

  1. Header — name, GitHub, LinkedIn, portfolio.
  2. Summary — role, years, and your strongest stack/specialisation.
  3. Experience — measurable, action-led bullets (impact, scale, performance).
  4. Projects — 2–3 with the tools used, your role and the result, with links.
  5. Skills — languages, frameworks, databases, cloud/DevOps.
  6. Education — degree and any relevant certifications.

Strong bullet examples

  • Reduced API response time 30% by adding caching and query optimisation.
  • Built a React design system adopted by 12 product teams, cutting UI build time 25%.
  • Shipped a CI pipeline that dropped deployment time from 40 to 8 minutes.
Mirror the exact technologies in the job posting, and quantify every bullet you can — numbers make impact believable.

Frequently asked questions

What should a software engineer resume include?
A summary with your tech stack, experience with measurable bullets, 2–3 projects with links, a skills list, and education — in a one-page ATS-friendly format.

What skills go on a developer resume?
Languages (e.g. Java, Python, JavaScript), frameworks (React, Node), databases (SQL, MongoDB), cloud/DevOps (AWS, Git, CI/CD) — matched to the job.

Should developers include projects?
Yes — 2–3 projects with the tools used, your role and the result (with GitHub/live links) are especially valuable for freshers.
