What is an ATS and why it matters

An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is software that scans, parses and ranks resumes before recruiters read them. If your resume isn’t ATS-friendly, it can be rejected automatically — no matter how qualified you are.

What an ATS checker looks at

  • Keywords — does your resume match the job description’s skills and terms?
  • Formatting — tables, columns, images and unusual fonts can break parsing.
  • Section headings — standard headings (Experience, Education, Skills) parse correctly.
  • Bullet strength — measurable, action-led bullets score higher.
  • Contact info & file type — a clean PDF with parseable text.
Fix the basics first: use a single-column layout, standard headings, real text (not images), and mirror the keywords from the job post.

Usually because of tables/columns/images that break parsing, non-standard headings, missing keywords, or scanned/image PDFs with no real text.
