Nurse Resume for India

Hospital recruiters and nursing superintendents look for a valid registration, the right clinical area and proof you can work safely under pressure. This guide shows how to present that clearly on one or two pages.

Updated October 2026 · By QuickResume · Free · No sign-up

Quick answer

Put your qualification and nursing council registration near the top, then list clinical experience by ward or unit (ICU, OT, emergency, general ward) with patient-load details, followed by certifications like BLS/ACLS and skills. Keep the layout plain and factual.

What hospitals check first

Nursing is a licensed profession, so employers verify credentials before anything else. A resume that makes these easy to find saves time for the reader and looks careful and professional.

Details to show clearly
  • Qualification: GNM, B.Sc Nursing, Post Basic B.Sc, or M.Sc Nursing, with institute and year
  • Registration: State Nursing Council registration number and its validity or renewal date
  • Clinical area: ICU, NICU, OT, emergency, medical-surgical ward, dialysis, labour room
  • Total experience and current employment status
  • Willingness for rotating shifts, if true

Never invent or round up a registration detail. Employers verify these, and an incorrect number can end an otherwise good application.

Shift flexibility is also practical information. If you can work night shifts, rotating duties or weekends, say so in one line. If you have personal limits, discuss them at the interview instead of writing them on the resume.

Best format and order of sections

Use a clean one-column format. Avoid photographs inside tables or decorative borders, because plain text is read more reliably by hospital HR software. For photo rules, see photo on resume in India.

  1. Header: name, phone, email, city.
  2. Professional summary: qualification, years, clinical area.
  3. Registration and certifications: council number, BLS, ACLS.
  4. Clinical experience: hospital, unit, bed strength, dates, bullets.
  5. Education: course, institute, university, year.
  6. Skills and training: procedures and equipment you are confident with.

Fresh GNM and B.Sc graduates can place Education above Experience and add clinical postings completed during training. Our resume format for freshers guide covers that approach.

Writing the summary

Keep the summary short and specific to your unit. Mention your area, years, and the type of setting such as a multispeciality hospital, a nursing home or a government facility.

Fictional example

B.Sc Nursing graduate with 4 years of ICU experience in a 300-bed multispeciality hospital. Skilled in ventilator care, central line care, medication administration and patient documentation. BLS certified; registered with a State Nursing Council. Comfortable with rotating night shifts.

Freshers can write: "Newly qualified GNM nurse with clinical training in medical, surgical and paediatric wards. Eager to learn in a supervised hospital setting."

Describe your clinical experience

Context matters in nursing. "Worked in ICU" tells little, but unit size, patient type and your responsibilities tell the reader what you can handle. Mention the number of beds in the unit, nurse-to-patient ratio if you know it, and the kinds of cases you managed. Do not include patient names or any identifying information. Patient privacy is part of professional ethics, and recruiters notice when a resume respects it.

Certifications, registration and training

List certificates with the issuing body and year. Include expiry if it applies, as BLS and ACLS typically need periodic renewal.

  • BLS (Basic Life Support): expected for most hospital roles
  • ACLS (Advanced Cardiac Life Support): valued in ICU, CCU and emergency
  • PALS or NRP: relevant for paediatric and neonatal units
  • Hospital training: infection control, fire safety, patient safety, NABH orientation if you have attended it
  • Specialty courses: critical care, dialysis, OT technique, diabetes education

If you are applying abroad, mention it lightly in one line such as "Preparing for IELTS/OET and the licensing exam of the target country". Requirements differ by country and change, so check the official regulator before applying.

Skills that read well

Clinical skills
  • IV cannulation, medication administration and infusion pump handling
  • Vital signs monitoring and early warning recognition
  • Wound care, catheterisation and Ryle's tube care
  • Ventilator and monitor basics (for ICU/OT)
  • Infection control and sterile technique
Soft skills
  • Clear handover communication
  • Calm decision-making in emergencies
  • Patient and family counselling
  • Teamwork with doctors and paramedical staff

List only the skills you have actually practised. Keep the list to eight or ten items so it stays readable. A line on languages is also useful, since patient communication in Hindi, English and a regional language is a real asset.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Missing registration details. This is the most common gap.
  • Long career history without units. Always name the unit.
  • Listing procedures you only observed. Write "assisted" or "observed" honestly.
  • Gaps left unexplained. Add a short line for breaks, such as maternity leave or further study.
  • Sending Word files with odd formatting. A clean PDF usually prints and opens the same everywhere; see PDF or Word resume.

For general structure, how to make a resume is a helpful companion guide. Before you send the resume, ask a senior colleague or batchmate to read it once. A second pair of eyes often catches a wrong date, a missing unit name or a typing error in your registration number.

Keep a scanned copy of your degree, registration and BLS certificate ready, since hospitals usually ask for them right after shortlisting.

Frequently asked questions

What should a nurse resume include?
Qualification, nursing council registration, clinical experience by unit, certifications such as BLS/ACLS, and a skills list focused on patient care.

Should I put my nursing registration number on my resume?
Yes. Add your State Nursing Council registration number and validity, as employers verify it early in hiring.

How can a fresher nurse write a resume?
Lead with your GNM or B.Sc Nursing details, then list clinical postings from training, internships and any certifications like BLS.

Is BLS necessary for nursing jobs?
Many hospitals expect it, and ACLS is valued for ICU and emergency roles. Always list the issuing body and year.

Can I use the same resume for hospitals in India and abroad?
Use the same facts but adjust format and wording for each regulator and employer, and check official requirements before applying.
