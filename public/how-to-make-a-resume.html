How to Make a Resume (Step by Step)

A recruiter-ready resume comes down to seven simple steps. Here’s exactly what to do — and a free way to build it without starting from a blank page.

Updated August 2026 · By QuickResume · Free · No sign-up

Quick answer

Pick an ATS-friendly template, add your header, a summary, work experience (with measurable bullets), education and skills, then proofread and export a PDF. Keep it to one page if you have under ~10 years’ experience.

7 steps to make a great resume

  1. Choose an ATS-friendly format — single column, standard headings, no images or tables.
  2. Add your header — name, phone, professional email, city, LinkedIn/portfolio.
  3. Write a summary or objective — 2–3 lines on your value (objective if you’re a fresher).
  4. List work experience — reverse-chronological, with action-led, measurable bullets.
  5. Add education — degree, institution, year, and CGPA if strong.
  6. Add skills — technical + tools, matched to the job description.
  7. Proofread and export — fix typos, keep to one page, save as PDF.
Tailor each resume to the job: mirror the keywords from the posting and lead with your most relevant achievements.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make a resume for the first time?
Start with an ATS-friendly template, then add your header, a short objective, education, projects, skills and any internships. QuickResume guides you through each step for free.

How long should a resume be?
One page for under ~10 years of experience; two pages only if you genuinely have the relevant experience to fill them.

What format should I save my resume in?
A PDF with real, selectable text — not a scanned image — so ATS software can read it.
