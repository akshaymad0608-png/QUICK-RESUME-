Biodata Format for Job (2026)

Many Indian job forms — government, PSU, and plenty of private employers — ask for a "biodata", not a resume. It looks similar but has a few sections a resume doesn't. Here's the exact format, plus a free way to build one.

Updated September 2026 · By QuickResume · Free · No sign-up

Quick answer

A biodata for a job has, in order: personal details (name, DOB, father's/husband's name, address, contact) → education → work experience or projects → skills → languages known → a short declaration with signature and date.

Biodata vs resume — the actual difference

A resume is built around your skills and achievements. A biodata additionally carries personal details a resume normally leaves out — this is the whole reason government forms, PSU recruitment portals, and some traditional employers ask for a "biodata" specifically instead of a resume.

The biodata format, section by section

  1. Personal details — full name, date of birth, father's/husband's name, gender, marital status, nationality, address, phone, email.
  2. Career objective — 2 lines on the role you want (optional but common on Indian biodata formats).
  3. Education — degree/board, institution, year, percentage or CGPA, most recent first.
  4. Work experience or projects — for experienced applicants, roles and responsibilities; for freshers, academic or personal projects.
  5. Skills — technical and soft skills relevant to the job.
  6. Languages known — languages you can read, write and speak.
  7. Declaration — "I hereby declare that the above information is true to the best of my knowledge," with place, date and signature.
Keep it to one page, use a plain font, and export as PDF unless the form specifically asks for a Word (.doc/.docx) file.

Biodata format for freshers

Same structure, with education and projects carrying the weight that work experience would for someone with a job history. Personal details, career objective, education, projects, skills, languages known, then the declaration — freshers should not skip the declaration line even without work experience to report.

Frequently asked questions

What is the biodata format for a job application?
Personal details (name, date of birth, father's/husband's name, address, contact) at the top, followed by education, work experience or projects, skills, languages known, and a declaration line with your signature and date.

Is biodata the same as a resume?
No. A resume focuses on skills and work achievements. A biodata additionally includes personal details — date of birth, gender, marital status, nationality, languages known — which is why government forms, PSU applications and some traditional employers ask for it by name specifically.

What is the biodata format for a fresher with no experience?
Same structure, with education and academic projects in place of the work experience section — personal details, education, projects, skills, languages known, then the declaration.
