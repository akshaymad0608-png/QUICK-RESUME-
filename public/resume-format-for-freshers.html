Resume Format for Freshers (2026)

A one-page structure that works when you have little or no work experience, with a sample and the mistakes to avoid.

Updated October 2026 · By QuickResume · Free · No sign-up

Quick answer

The best resume format for freshers is one page, single column, in this order: contact details → short objective → education → projects → internships → skills → certifications. With no work history, projects and education do the heavy lifting.

The resume format for freshers, section by section

A fresher has less work history, so the order changes: education and projects move up and carry the weight that job experience would for someone older. Keep to a single column and one page.

#SectionWhat to write
1Contact detailsName, phone, professional email, city, and a LinkedIn or GitHub link if you have one
2Career objective / summary2 to 3 lines (about 30 to 50 words): the role you want, your strongest skills, what you can contribute
3EducationLatest first. Degree, college or university, year, CGPA or percentage. Many Indian recruiters also expect 12th and 10th details
4Projects2 to 3 projects: what you built, the tools used, and the result or what you learned
5Internships / trainingRole, organisation, dates and 2 to 3 bullet points of what you did
6Skills8 to 12 relevant skills, grouped (for example technical, tools, soft skills)
7Certifications and achievementsCourses, competitions, college activities, anything with a name and a date
Not applying for a job but filling a government or PSU form that asks for a "biodata"? That is a slightly different format, covered in our biodata format guide.

Fresher resume with no experience: what to put instead

  • Projects: college, personal or open-source work. Name the tools and say what the project did.
  • Internships and training: even a short one counts if you can say what you did.
  • Certifications: relevant online courses with the platform and year.
  • College activities: clubs, events, volunteering or leadership, in one line each.
  • Skills the job asks for: use the wording from the job posting where it is true for you.

Sample fresher resume (fictional, for illustration)

Your Name · +91 98XXXXXXXX · yourname@email.com · Pune · linkedin.com/in/yourname

Objective: Final-year B.Tech (IT) student with hands-on Python and SQL project experience, looking for a junior data analyst role where I can turn raw data into clear reports.

Education: B.Tech, Information Technology, XYZ University, 2022 to 2026, CGPA 8.1 · 12th (Science), ABC Junior College, 2022, 82% · 10th, ABC High School, 2020, 88%

Projects: Sales dashboard (Python, pandas, Power BI): cleaned 20,000 rows and built a monthly report that cut manual work from hours to minutes.

Internship: Data intern, ABC Pvt Ltd (Jun to Aug 2025): prepared weekly reports in Excel and SQL for the operations team.

Skills: Python, SQL, Excel, Power BI, communication, problem-solving

The names, numbers and companies above are made up. Replace them with your own real details, and never add a skill, mark or project you cannot talk about in an interview.

Formatting rules that keep it ATS-friendly

  • Single column, standard headings (Education, Projects, Skills), no tables or text boxes for the main content.
  • Text-based PDF (you can select the text). Our ATS resume guide explains why.
  • No photo unless the application asks for one. Many ATS tools cannot read images, and a photo takes space from your content.
  • Readable font (about 10 to 12 pt) and consistent date format.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best resume format for freshers?
A one-page, single-column resume that puts education and projects before work history: contact details, a short objective, education, projects, internships, skills, then certifications. Keep it plain so applicant tracking software can read it.

How do I make a resume with no experience?
Lead with education and projects, then add internships, certifications and college activities. Describe each project by what you built, the tools you used and the result. Use the skills the job posting asks for, as long as they are true.

Should a fresher add 10th and 12th marks?
Many Indian recruiters, especially for campus placements and government or PSU roles, expect them, so a short education line for each is common. For some private-sector jobs your degree and projects matter more. Follow what the job posting asks for.

Should a fresher resume be one page?
Yes. One page is the standard for freshers. If you are struggling to fill it, you probably do not need a second page.

Should I put a photo on my resume?
Only if the application specifically asks for one. A photo is not needed for most private-sector jobs and some ATS tools cannot read it.
